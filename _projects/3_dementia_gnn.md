We developed protein–protein interaction-aware graph propagation networks that identify neuroimaging- and neuropathology-driven subtypes of dementia and mild cognitive impairment from plasma proteins.
Building on this, we are developing multimodal models that integrate gut microbiome, genetic variants, plasma proteins, and neuroimaging for Alzheimer's disease patient stratification.

**Disease:** Alzheimer's disease, dementia, MCI · **Data:** plasma proteomics, neuroimaging, microbiome · **Methods:** GNN, explainable AI
