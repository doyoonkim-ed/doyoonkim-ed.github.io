Metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease (MASLD) progresses very differently across patients.
By integrating whole-exome sequencing and bulk transcriptome data from patient cohorts, we identified _IFI16_ variants and expression as markers of MASLD progression.
This work was published in _Hepatology_ (2025) and led to a registered Korean patent.

**Disease:** MASLD · **Data:** WES, bulk RNA-seq · **Methods:** multi-omics integration
