- **Intrahepatic cholangiocarcinoma:** integrating single-cell multiome data (scRNA-seq, scATAC-seq) to map the cell composition and regulatory networks of the tumor immune microenvironment (TIME), and to find epi-driver candidates that regulate it.
- **Hepatocellular carcinoma:** combining Visium spatial transcriptomics with scRNA/snRNA-seq to resolve intratumoral heterogeneity and the spatial distribution of the immune microenvironment.

This direction is supported by a Ministry of Education / NRF Basic Research Program grant (PI, 2026–2029).

**Disease:** ICC, HCC · **Data:** scRNA-seq, scATAC-seq, snRNA-seq, Visium · **Methods:** spatial deconvolution, multi-omics integration
