At Cedars-Sinai Medical Center, I am integrating 26 public omics, clinical, and pharmacological knowledge bases into a unified biomedical knowledge graph, and building **HybridRAG-AD**, a knowledge graph-augmented LLM system for evidence-grounded reasoning in Alzheimer's disease.
The same graph will be used to prioritize targets and pathways in liver cancer omics analyses.

**Disease:** Alzheimer's disease, liver cancer · **Data:** 26 public knowledge bases · **Methods:** knowledge graph, GraphRAG, LLM
